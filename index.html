ALINEA Creator Affiliate Program

By applying to the Creator Affiliate Program through Lemon Squeezy, you confirm you've read this page and agree to the terms below. If anything here is unclear, message us before applying.

1. How commission is attributed.

You earn commission only when a sale happens through your unique affiliate link — the one tied to your account in Lemon Squeezy. Lemon Squeezy creates an anonymous tracking identifier when someone clicks your link, and that identifier stays active for 365 days. Any sale made within that window, through that link, is yours.

If someone hears about ALINEA from your video, your bio, or a code you mentioned, but enters the site on their own — without clicking your link — and completes a purchase, no referral is created and no commission is owed to you. This isn't unique to ALINEA: it's how affiliate tracking works everywhere. The best way to protect your commission is to make your link the easiest, most obvious thing to click — in your caption, your pinned comment, or your bio while your content is live.

2. The SPARK69 discount and your link are two different things.

The discount code gives buyers $20 off; your affiliate ID is what attributes the sale to you. Your personal link — sent to you once approved — combines both. Using a link that only carries the discount, or only your affiliate ID, will not give your audience the full experience: always use the link we send you.

3. Commission structure.

Standard commission is $15 per sale — more than 20% of the product's price. There is no cap on total earnings and no fine print beyond what's written here. Lemon Squeezy measures every sale automatically; we don't track it manually and we don't decide who gets paid — Lemon Squeezy does, based on its own referral data.

4. Approval and payout.

After applying, Lemon Squeezy reviews your profile (bio, social links, estimated monthly views). Validating the bank account or PayPal you register for payouts typically takes 1 day, 2 at most. If significantly more time passes with no update, it's worth resubmitting your application. Once approved, we send you your combined link directly — it does not appear automatically inside your Lemon Squeezy dashboard.

5. What we expect from your content.

One genuine video is enough to participate — more is welcome, never required. Content should reflect your actual experience with ALINEA: your galaxy, your books, your way of creating. It should not be scripted to feel like a commercial, and it should not misrepresent what ALINEA does. ALINEA is a personal knowledge tool — your library, your highlights, and the connections between what you've read, kept locally on your device. It is not a marketplace for free or pirated books, and content suggesting otherwise is not permitted.

6. No risk, no penalty.

If your content doesn't generate sales, nothing happens to your affiliate account or your standing with us. You're welcome to try a different angle or format at your own pace.

7. Free Pro access.

If you want to try ALINEA Pro before posting anything, message us directly — on Instagram or by email — and, if it's a genuine fit, we'll set you up with full access at no cost. This is separate from, and does not require, your affiliate application.

8. Sharing your content with us.

Once your video is published, we ask that you share the link and basic metrics (views, likes) with us. We may repost or share your content, tagging your account, for additional exposure — we will not do this without the content being yours and public.

9. No exclusivity, no commitment.

Joining the program doesn't bind you to ALINEA exclusively, and you can stop participating at any time. There's no minimum activity required to keep your affiliate account active.

10. Payments and the role of Lemon Squeezy.

All payments — what your audience pays, and what you earn — are processed by Lemon Squeezy, acting as the payment platform for both sides. ALINEA does not handle, hold, or move your commission manually at any point.

11. The earnings calculator is a projection, not a guarantee.

The numbers you see in the calculator are estimates based on general conversion benchmarks, not a real or guaranteed figure. Your actual results may land close to these projections, above them, or below them — there is no assurance that your earnings will match what the calculator shows. Use it as a planning reference, not as a promise.
